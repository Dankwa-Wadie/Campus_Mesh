# Campus Mesh product direction

## Promise
Your campus, connected: campus conversations, private messages, and nearby communication when internet access is limited. Range depends on available peers and supported transports; do not promise campus-wide coverage or emergency delivery.

## Daily value
Prioritize catching up on campus channels, contacting a peer, and finding nearby conversations. Privacy and mesh networking support these everyday jobs. Keep technical configuration behind an understandable connection summary.

## Home screen upgrade
Clear message and explore actions, connected-peer information, conversation-name search, All / Unread / Direct / Channels filters, readable rows, and useful empty states. Retains blue and gold branding and theme support. Search covers existing conversation names, not message history. Pinned shortcuts appear in the default view; filtered results contain existing conversations only.

## Next increments
1. Delivery clarity: show queued, sent, and confirmed states from actual transport evidence, with actionable retry and connection explanations.
2. Campus context: explain public channels, announcement posting rights, and verified roles; support explicit campus selection without exposing private identity unnecessarily.
3. Study groups: pilot course and study channels before adding feeds, marketplaces, or events. Validate repeat usefulness with students.
4. Guided discovery: explain the reach of Nearby versus internet-backed channels, and provide permission recovery where needed.
5. Conversation polish: clear public/private labels, accessible attachments, and unmistakable microphone activity during voice use.

## Validation
Check small screens, large fonts, light/dark themes, long names, empty states, keyboard behavior, and navigation. Use two devices to verify discovery, reconnects, messaging, and voice. Evaluate time to first successful message and whether students understand who receives a message. Do not collect message content for analytics.
